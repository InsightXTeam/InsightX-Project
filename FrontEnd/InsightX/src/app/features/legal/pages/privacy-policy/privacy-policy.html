LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 8, 2026 • Effective Date: July 8, 2026

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to InsightX AI ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your enterprise data, departmental metrics, and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and safeguard data when your organization uses our business intelligence, automated KPI tracking, anomaly detection, and AI assistant services (collectively, the "Platform").

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in three primary categories to provide and improve our enterprise analytics platform:

  • Account & Identity Data: When an organization registers or when an Owner or Super Admin invites team members, we collect names, business email addresses, company names, role designations (SuperAdmin, Owner, Manager), and encrypted authentication credentials.
  • Departmental & KPI Metric Data: To deliver real-time business intelligence, we store and process the key performance indicators (KPIs), metric targets, historical performance values, departmental hierarchy structures, and custom reporting formulas submitted to your company workspace.
  • System Usage & Diagnostic Logs: We automatically collect telemetry data, such as IP addresses, browser types, session durations, anomaly alert triggers, and API response latencies, to monitor system reliability and security.

3. How We Use AI & Automated Processing

InsightX AI utilizes advanced artificial intelligence and statistical machine learning algorithms to analyze your KPI data. Here is how our AI engine interacts with your data:

🤖 AI Data Isolation Guarantee

Your company's departmental data and KPI metrics are processed strictly within your isolated tenant workspace. We never use your confidential enterprise data, chat queries, or report summaries to train public AI models or share them with third-party organizations.

  • Autonomous Anomaly Detection: Our AI algorithms scan metric trends in real time to identify abnormal spikes or drops and dispatch instant notifications to authorized Managers and Owners.
  • Conversational AI Assistant: When you query our AI Chat, your prompt and relevant context metrics are processed in memory to generate natural-language root-cause explanations and strategic advice.
  • Automated Report Accuracy Verification: Our system verifies monthly report summaries against raw metric logs to calculate an accuracy score and prevent AI hallucinations.

4. Data Sharing & Sub-Processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or enterprise data. We only share information under the following strict circumstances:

  • Within Your Organization: Data is accessible according to your role-based access permissions (e.g., Owners can view all departmental KPIs, while Managers access assigned areas).
  • Cloud & Infrastructure Providers: We use industry-leading, SOC-2 compliant cloud hosting and database providers (such as Amazon Web Services and Google Cloud) to securely host your data in encrypted environments.
  • Legal & Regulatory Requirements: We may disclose information if required by applicable law, subpoena, or government regulation, provided we notify your organization whenever legally permitted.

5. Data Security & Retention

We employ robust administrative, technical, and physical security controls:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 encryption.
  • Token-Based Authentication: We utilize secure JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with short-lived access tokens and automatic refresh rotation to prevent unauthorized session access.
  • Retention Policy: We retain enterprise KPI data and account logs for as long as your company workspace remains active. Upon account deactivation or formal deletion request, all tenant data is permanently purged from our active servers within 30 days.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR in Europe or CCPA/CPRA in California), you and your organization possess specific rights regarding your data:

  • Access & Portability: You can export your company's KPI reports and historical data directly from the platform at any time.
  • Correction & Modification: Company Owners and Managers can modify departmental profiles, user roles, and metric entries directly through the dashboard.
  • Account Deactivation & Deletion: Owners may request complete workspace termination and data erasure by contacting our privacy team or via account settings.
